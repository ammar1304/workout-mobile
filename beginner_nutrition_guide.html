Easy high-protein meal ideas
Breakfast
~35 g protein
  • 4 whole eggs scrambled + 1 cup oats with milk
  • Greek yogurt + banana + handful of almonds
  • Protein smoothie: milk + whey + oats + peanut butter
Lunch
~40 g protein
  • 200 g chicken breast + rice + vegetables
  • Tuna or sardines + whole wheat bread + salad
  • Lentil soup + 2 boiled eggs + wholegrain bread
Dinner
~45 g protein
  • 200 g beef or salmon + sweet potato + broccoli
  • Turkey mince + pasta + tomato sauce + cheese
  • Grilled fish + quinoa + roasted vegetables
Snacks
~20 g protein
  • Cottage cheese + fruit
  • Whey protein shake + milk
  • Hard-boiled eggs + a piece of fruit
  • Peanut butter on rice cakes
What to eat around your workout
Pre-workout
1–2 hours before
Carbs for energy + moderate protein
Oats + milk + banana
Rice + chicken (if eating 2 hrs before)
Yogurt + fruit (30 min before, light)
Avoid high fat or very heavy meals — slows digestion
Post-workout
Within 1–2 hours after
Protein to repair muscle + carbs to refuel
Whey protein shake + banana (fast)
Chicken + rice + vegetables (full meal)
Greek yogurt + oats + honey
Aim for 30–40 g protein in this window
Hydration matters too — drink at least 2–3 litres of water daily, and add an extra 500 ml for every hour of training. Dehydration directly reduces strength output.
